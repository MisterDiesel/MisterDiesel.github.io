WHO WE ARE

The No-Gatekeeping Collective

REDLINE Collective was born out of a simple idea: car culture should be about community, not competition or cost.

Too often, car clubs feel exclusive, elitist, or limited to specific vehicle classes. In Medicine Hat, we wanted to break those barriers down. Whether you have a track-prepped build, a project car that's half-finished, or a bone-stock hatchback that gets you to work, you are a car enthusiast. That's all that matters to us.

To fund our community meets, cruises, and events, we design and produce high-quality, durable, and affordable apparel and decals. Every purchase goes directly back into hosting free public events, booking meet locations, and keeping the collective rolling.

All Vehicles Welcome

No application screens or vehicle vetting. If you love cars, you are in.

High-Quality, Low-Cost Merch

Sourced from high-grade materials and priced fairly, so you look great without breaking the bank.

100% Community Funded

Every dollar generated goes right back into supporting local meetups, photo sessions, and cruises.
